Business rules — Monthly Trend (§9.1)
Each bar segment = count of distinct MJs who completed at least 1 job in that role during the month.
An MJ active in 2 roles (e.g., Chaperone + Befriender) is counted once per role in this chart, but once in the unique MJ total.
Trend reflects completed jobs only. Cancelled and at-risk jobs are excluded. Confirmed-but-not-completed jobs are also excluded until check-out is recorded.
Chart always shows the full year. Use the Month filter to scope the KPI cards, donut and table to a specific period.

MJ Count
Unique MJs — completed jobs
"No. of MJ" = distinct MJs with ≥1 completed job in the period. MJ engagement = ≥1 job/month (monthly) or ≥3 jobs/quarter (quarterly).

Hours
MJ attendance time (not SCC billing)
Hours = actual check-in to check-out per MJ. SCC client billing is based on scheduled slot — these differ. Rounding: first hour minimum; ≥0.50h additional rounds to next full hour.
