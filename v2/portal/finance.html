Business Rules — MJ Payment (§1.2, §7)
Basis: MJ payment uses actual check-in / check-out time, NOT scheduled time. SCC client billing uses scheduled time.
Minimum: 1 hour for the first hour. After first hour: every 30+ minutes rounds up to the next full hour.
Rounding: 1h 0m–29m = 1 hr · 1h 30m–59m = 2 hrs · 2h 30m–59m = 3 hrs, etc.
Amendment: If actual time exceeds scheduled, SCC staff must amend in CARES. Flagged rows require sign-off before AP export.

Export: AP listing exported as Excel for HR/Finance processing. Status transitions: Draft → Submitted → Processed.
